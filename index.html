Why These Prototypes Matter for This Assignment

  • Direct TOR interpretation capability: each prototype was built in response to a real development bank TOR — CC+/GCF, GCF DOMCREP, World Bank UBEC P171833, and IDB BL-T1194. Building them required reading and interpreting the specifications, not just describing them.
  • Results framework literacy in practice: building these prototypes required understanding how indicators are defined, how baselines and targets are set, how means of verification are operationalised, and how reporting cycles function within a development bank framework.
  • Climate and gender integration as a data model: the GCFT Portal prototype demonstrates that I understand climate and gender tagging as a data architecture, not as a narrative device — directly applicable to the cross-cutting integration requirements of Pillar 2.
  • Institutional sustainability logic: each prototype was designed with handover to a national institution in mind — the same sustainability logic required in the Pillar 2 project design.
  • Regional replication as a design principle: the GCFT Portal prototype is designed to serve multiple SIDS jurisdictions, demonstrating the replication architecture the Pillar 2 design must also address.
  • Honest positioning: I am not claiming deployed, operational systems. I am claiming the capability to interpret a TOR and build a system that responds to it. That capability is what the Pillar 2 assignment requires.
